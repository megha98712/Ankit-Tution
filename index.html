About Us

Ankit Tuition is a trusted learning center located in Ganesh Nagar, Dahisar (E), Mumbai. We provide quality education and personal guidance for students from Junior KG to 8th Standard. Our goal is to build strong concepts, confidence, and curiosity in every student through interactive teaching methods, regular tests, and individual attention.

At Ankit Tuition, we believe in motivating students to achieve excellence in academics and develop life skills for a brighter future.

Features

Personal Attention to Every Student

Each student gets individual focus to strengthen their weak areas and build confidence in learning.

Small Batch Size

We maintain limited students per batch for effective communication and doubt clearance.

Experienced and Dedicated Teachers

Highly skilled tutors with years of experience guide students with modern teaching techniques.

Regular Doubt Solving Sessions

Special sessions are conducted to clear students’ doubts and ensure strong concept clarity.

Weekly Tests and Reports

Regular tests and progress reports help track student performance and improvement areas.

Interactive and Fun Learning

Learning is made interesting with activities, visuals, and real-life examples for better understanding.

Homework Assistance

Students get complete guidance for their homework and project work on a daily basis.

Parent-Teacher Meetings

Regular communication with parents to discuss academic progress and areas of improvement.

Affordable Fees

Providing top-quality education at reasonable fees to make learning accessible for everyone.
